## Effective date

Art. 54 of the official form states that the law enters into force one year after publication in the Monitorul Oficial, except for art. 42 alin. (7). The official republication note confirms the original publication date as 21.03.2006. The general effective date is therefore calculated deterministically as:

`2006-03-21 + 1 year = 2007-03-21`

This field represents the general rule; the explicit exception for art. 42 alin. (7) is documented here and is not modeled as a separate scoped temporal field.
