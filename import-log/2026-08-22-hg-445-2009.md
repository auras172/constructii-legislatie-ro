## Effective date and repeal status

Article 36 of HG 445/2009 states that the hotărâre enters into force 60 days after publication. With the official publication date 2009-07-13, the effective date is calculated deterministically as 2009-09-11.

The official Legea nr. 292/2018 record is already present locally and was also checked in the Portal browser. Art. 32 lit. c) expressly abrogates HG nr. 445/2009 at the date that Legea 292/2018 enters into force. Art. 34 states that Legea 292/2018 enters into force 30 days after publication; its official publication is M.Of. nr. 1043/10.12.2018, giving the deterministic repeal date 2019-01-09. The metadata therefore uses `status: repealed`. No `repeals`, `repealed_by` or `repeal_date` field/edge is added because the repository schema/task scope does not support it.
